wewi Privacy Policy
Version: 2026-10-05 · Effective: October 5, 2026

1. Operator and contact
elixirevo is responsible for wewi and support handled by the operator. Privacy questions, access, correction, deletion and objections: elixirevo@gmail.com (elixirevo, privacy contact). Website: https://github.com/elixirevo/wewi. Current documents are in Settings > Help & Support and prior versions remain in repository history. This policy covers wewi; websites you open have separate policies.

2. Widget data on your Mac
wewi stores widget identifiers, names, URLs, positions, sizes, opacity, enabled/interaction state, refresh intervals, website modes, mode-change reload preferences and saved scroll positions in the app's UserDefaults (wewi.widgets.v1). These settings are used to provide and restore the widgets you request. URLs can contain personal information or access tokens; avoid embedding secrets in them. wewi does not upload the widget list or page contents to the operator.
WebKit loads websites you choose. Requests expose your IP address, URL, browser headers and any data you enter to those sites and their embedded services. Cookies, login sessions, caches and website storage may persist in WebKit's default data store on your Mac and may be shared between widgets. A widget may contact its website while wewi runs in the background, including on automatic refresh. Disable or delete the widget, or quit wewi, to stop its loading. Deleting a widget removes its configuration but does not clear shared website data. Settings > Features > the widget’s menu > Clear Website Cookies lets you remove cookies for the configured and currently open hosts, including applicable parent-domain cookies. This may sign out other widgets sharing those cookies; it does not remove cached files or local storage. Open sites can recreate cookies. Removing the app alone may leave preferences, WebKit data and backups behind. Sign out of websites and manage site data and your Mac's files/backups separately.

3. Preferences and agreement records
Language, appearance, grid snapping, onboarding completion and crash-report preference are kept locally until removed from the app's preferences. The terms receipt contains document ID, version, displayed language, acceptance time and a SHA-256 hash of the displayed text. It stays locally until replaced by acceptance of another version or deletion of app preferences; it is not a server audit record. These records provide your selected settings and remember the agreement. Restore Defaults only restores appearance and does not erase widgets, site data, consent or login settings.
wewi does not need Accessibility or Screen Recording permission for desktop widgets. Launch at login is registered with macOS only when you enable it. External websites may have their own permission requests.

4. Optional crash reporting
Crash reporting is OFF by default. You can choose it in onboarding or Settings > General. The choice is separate from Terms acceptance. Changes take effect after quitting and relaunching wewi; turning it off does not stop the already running SDK until you quit.
When enabled for a launch, Sentry processes fatal error reports containing exception details, technical call stacks, application version/build, operating system and device information to diagnose crashes. User fields, HTTP requests, arbitrary extra fields, tags, breadcrumbs, messages and server names are removed before sending. Usage analytics, session tracking, performance tracing, screen recording and replay are disabled. Technical exception text may still contain identifying information; reports are not guaranteed anonymous. Receivers necessarily handle network connection information. Project-side IP scrubbing and default sensitive-data scrubbing are enabled.
Pending crash envelopes can be stored in Sentry's local cache (up to 20 items; this is not a time-based retention guarantee). Disabling reporting does not delete that cache or reports already received. No SDK is initialized on launches with reporting off; re-enabling it later may send pending reports. Contact us to request deletion of received reports. macOS diagnostics controlled by Apple's own settings are separate.

5. Updates and support
Sparkle checks https://github.com/elixirevo/wewi/releases/latest/download/appcast.xml and downloads release assets from GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. The receiving servers can process IP addresses, requested paths and app/framework HTTP headers for delivery and security. Optional system profiling is disabled. Automatic checks are enabled by default in this distribution and can be disabled in Settings > Software Update. Existing user preferences are retained. Manual checks and downloads also make requests; automatic installation is not enabled by the app's default configuration.
Opening support links or composing email does not send a report automatically. If you send email to elixirevo@gmail.com, Google processes the email and the operator receives your address, message and attachments to answer your request. GitHub issues are public: your account name, issue and attachments can be seen by others. Send only information needed for the request. Support messages are used to handle the request and associated follow-up or disputes; request deletion by email when no longer needed. Any legally required preservation is limited to the relevant records and obligation.
The support diagnostics preview contains only app name, version/build, OS version and distribution channel. Copying or saving it does not send it to us. You decide whether to attach it to a request.

6. Recipients, international processing and retention
Optional crashes: Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry), United States ingestion region, for crash storage and analysis. Reports are sent over HTTPS during a consented run or a later consented launch. The organization's current Trial plan retains error events for 90 days (verified October 5, 2026). Retention for new events can change with the active plan; the free Developer plan retains errors for 30 days. We will update this notice when that setting changes. Local cache retention is described above. Contact the operator for deletion requests. Service privacy/contact: https://sentry.io/privacy/.
Updates and public issues: GitHub, Inc. and its service infrastructure, which can process information in the United States and other countries. GitHub determines its delivery/security log retention under https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. wewi does not set a separate fixed lifetime for those server logs.
Email support: Google LLC and its service infrastructure may process messages in the United States and other countries under https://policies.google.com/privacy. Mail delivery and provider backup retention follow that service's policies. The operator cannot promise immediate deletion from independent provider backups.
Websites in your widgets: the operators and countries depend on the URLs and embedded content you choose. Their own notices describe their processing and retention. The operator does not receive all website traffic through a wewi server.
You can refuse optional crash transfers by leaving reporting off, refuse update connections by disabling checks and not requesting downloads, and avoid support transfers by not sending a message. Core widgets do not require crash-report or support consent. Websites require network connections to display their content. Where additional notices or consent are legally required, Terms acceptance alone does not replace them.

7. Safeguards and deletion
The app limits reporting through the shared crash-only adapter and uses HTTPS for configured Sentry and update destinations. Website transport depends on the URL you enter; HTTP sites are not encrypted by wewi. Local preferences are not separately encrypted by wewi. Your macOS account and device protections govern access to local data. Data held by the operator is deleted when no longer needed for the purpose or applicable legal obligations, including in response to valid deletion requests. Provider-controlled backups and copies you make require separate handling. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens in support requests.

8. Your rights
Email the privacy contact to request access/copies, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal where applicable. We seek only the minimum information needed to identify the relevant record and verify your authority, explain refusals and receive appeals at the same address. We cannot remotely delete data stored only on your Mac or locate a report that cannot be associated with your request. You may also contact Korea's privacy infringement center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr) or dispute mediation committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr).
Applicable US state rights may include portable copies, deletion, correction and appeals. Availability depends on the law and its applicability. wewi does not use its own advertising, sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or perform profiling for legally significant decisions. It has no separate GPC/DNT processing mechanism; websites in widgets independently handle browser signals. We do not unlawfully discriminate for exercising rights.

9. Age and changes
wewi is a general utility intended for adults, not a child-directed service. If you believe a child supplied personal information to the operator, contact us for appropriate handling. Policy changes and effective dates are announced in release notes and bundled documents; a new purpose requiring consent is presented before that processing begins. A policy update or Terms agreement does not automatically enable crash reporting.
